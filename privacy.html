Opickles Privacy Notice

Lalita's Collection (brand: Opickles) · Last updated 13 October 2026

This notice explains how we handle information when you buy Opickles products on Amazon.in.

What we receive

When you order from us on Amazon, Amazon shares the details we need to deliver your order: the products, your name, delivery address and phone number. We do not receive your payment details.

How we use it

  • To pack and ship your order (shipping label, packing slip and courier pickup through Amazon Easy Ship).
  • To issue the GST tax invoice required by law.
  • To understand sales in aggregate (for example, which cities or states order most). This uses only city, state and pincode.

We never use your details for marketing, never sell them, and never share them with anyone other than Amazon and its delivery partners.

How we protect it

  • Your name, address and phone are encrypted (AES-256) in our order system, which runs only on our own computer and is not reachable from the internet.
  • Only the business owner can see your details. Every access is logged and the logs are reviewed every two weeks.
  • We run automatic security scans every month and before every software update.

How long we keep it

Your name, address, phone and email are deleted automatically within 30 days after your order is shipped. We keep order numbers and amounts as required by tax law, without your contact details.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Contact us through Amazon's Buyer–Seller Messaging, or email ankitjhunjhunwala03@gmail.com.
